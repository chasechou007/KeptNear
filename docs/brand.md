# KeptNear Brand

**Approved public descriptor:** Local Password & Token Manager

**中文副标题：** 本地密码与令牌管理器

KeptNear is the public product name. `PSW`, `PSWMac`, the existing bundle
identifier, and the `.pswvault` extension remain internal compatibility
identifiers until a separate migration is designed and tested.

The pre-alpha App and README now use the approved descriptor because typed
token storage, Apps & Tools, Broker capabilities, MCP, and CLI are implemented
and tested in source. Public copy must still distinguish source implementation,
components bundled without service activation, and released end-user
capabilities.

## Mark

The mark is built from two independent folded leaves held close together with a
deliberate narrow gap.

- The folded form represents a portable encrypted vault file.
- The paired leaves represent local copies carried by a user-selected sync
  provider.
- The gap is essential: synchronization keeps copies near without transferring
  custody to KeptNear.

The mark must not be redrawn as a lock, key, shield, cloud, fingerprint,
checkmark, monogram, or closed container. Keep the proportions, center gap, and
left-to-right color order unchanged.

## Palette

| Role | Light appearance | Dark appearance | Use |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Primary | Juniper `#246B5E` | Sea Glass `#65C2AE` | Controls, focus, selection, left leaf |
| Secondary | Coral `#D9684A` | Coral Light `#E07A5F` | Right leaf and restrained brand accents |
| Graphite | `#202724` | Mist `#F3F5F1` | Wordmark and folded leaf backs |
| Paper | `#FBFCFA` | System background | Exported artwork only |
| Mist | `#F3F5F1` | System separator | Exported artwork and subtle neutral surfaces |

Application surfaces should continue using native macOS semantic backgrounds,
materials, labels, separators, warning colors, and error colors. Brand colors
do not replace semantic status colors. Juniper on white has a WCAG contrast
ratio of `6.29:1`; Sea Glass on Graphite has a ratio of `7.18:1`. Coral remains
decorative unless its specific foreground/background pairing is checked.
